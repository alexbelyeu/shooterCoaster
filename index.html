ShooterCoaster

ShooterCoaster is a free browser rail shooter: you ride a rollercoaster through five themed worlds and shoot everything that moves before the timer runs out.

Five worlds

  • Theme Park: pop balloons past the Ferris wheel, then bring down the Golden Blimp.
  • Desert: scorpions and flocks of crows around the pyramids.
  • Ocean: jellyfish, sharks and schools of flying fish.
  • Antarctica: snowman scouts, snowmen and brutes on the ice.
  • Inferno: ember wisps, fire imps, lava golems and drakes around an erupting volcano.
  • Climbing Gym: a coaster threaded through a giant indoor climbing hall, with climbers racing up the walls to top out before you shoot them off.

How to play

  • The coaster drives itself. You aim and shoot.
  • On a computer, aim with the mouse and click to fire. On a phone or tablet, tap an enemy to shoot it.
  • Chain kills quickly to build a combo, up to a five times multiplier and FRENZY slow motion.
  • Every ride is a 60-second run: waves from the first second, building to a finale that ends it. Score enough for up to three stars; a star unlocks the next world.

Free, no download

Free to play, with no download, no install and no account. It runs in any modern browser on a desktop, laptop, phone or tablet. ShooterCoaster is made with three.js and React Three Fiber, and its worlds, enemies, music and sound effects are all generated in code.
