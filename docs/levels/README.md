# Level blockouts

`src/levels/environments/biomes/gym/blockout.json` is Alex's blockout of his real climbing gym
(12 Nov 2026, #513), built from the gym's Setter's Map and his photos; keep it verbatim. Level 6 is
built from it: the pure `blockout.ts` next to it reads it as typed pieces (their solid boxes, the
named anchors: pit, star, Arch opening, Flame, Tree face, glass room windows) and scales it to the
level (`GYM_SCALE`, 48 world units per metre: the 80 m hall is 3840 units long). Don't name the real
gym anywhere: in the game it is "the Climbing Gym".

## The ride (#514, rebuilt in #700)

`gymTrack.ts` builds the level's ride from the ride kit's storyboard (`docs/RIDE-KIT.md` "Level 6"): the game's
first **v2 ride** (#696: gravity with drag, speed zones, beats) and the first on special mechanics (#699), laid out
element by element by the ride elements' turtle (#698) in blockout metres round the blockout's anchors. The
storyboard, with its seconds, is the comment at the top of `gymTrack.ts`: the station by the entrance and GO's launch
down the black path at the Flame (0 s), a zero-g roll over the fire (4 s), a head-chopper under the Arch (8 s), an
airtime hill past the cubbies (12 s), the Training Area's cave (15 s), the route choice across the hall (#702: the V8
way round swings east over the Flame's pads, 19–21 s), the wall ride up Alaska to just under the trusses (22 s), the
dyno's jump gap to the Shield (30 s), the auto-belay drop into the pit (31.5 s), the Medusa helix: a climbing turn round
over the hall's floor that comes out facing the star as she rises (40 s) and a chain run up the hall at her face
(40–44 s), the dive round her shoulder, along the Tree's roots and the hairpin in its left cave (45 s), a heartline roll through
the Tree's gap (55 s) and the brake run (58 s); a chain brings the car home round the hall's west side after the run.
`gymTrack.test.ts` holds every beat to its second (±0.5 s, solved by `rideV2.ts`), the roll inverted over the Flame,
the Arch's near miss (measured), the wall ride under the trusses, the mechanics, the star on screen for 4 s from the Medusa's
rise, and the clearances: the blockout, every triangle the hall draws (#695), the track itself, the junction's branch,
and the Medusa's bust leant out or not. The intro's tour reveals the pit from mid-hall, pulls back down the hall
facing the Starr and rides the last 12 m of track into the station (`hero.approach`).
`gym-track.svg` here is its plot (re-draw it with `node scripts/plot-gym-track.mjs docs/levels/gym-track.svg`).
The level registry is startup JS, so it doesn't import the blockout: it takes the track and the
climbed walls (`walls.ts`) from `gymLevel.ts`, plain numbers generated by `node scripts/gen-gym-level.mjs`.
After changing the blockout, the ride or the walls, re-run it: `gymLevel.test.ts` fails while it is stale (#589).
Level 1 does the same (#863): its ride comes from `parkLevel.ts`, written by `node scripts/gen-park-level.mjs`
after any change to `parkTrack.ts` (`parkLevel.test.ts` fails while it is stale), and level 3's (#865) from `oceanLevel.ts`,
written by `node scripts/gen-ocean-level.mjs` after any change to `oceanTrack.ts` (with the sea arch; `oceanLevel.test.ts`). The generators round the numbers
to fit the startup JS budget, so the ride tests (`parkTrack.test.ts`) check the shipped track as well as the authored one.
`gym-ride-moments.jpg` is the ride from the rider's seat before #659's re-route (#544; `?e2e=1`, `rideTo` 0, 0.1, 0.45, 0.55,
0.65, 0.85, left to right, top to bottom): up the Tree, at the Flame, past the Wave (its contact
shadow on the mats), through the Arch, by the glass gym room, the run-in to the Starr.

## Axes and units

Metres. Hall 80 × 40 × 27 (x × z × height). `x` runs the long way, 0 at the Starr end
(north) → 80 at the south end; `z` runs across, 0 on the east side (the Tree, the Training
Area, the Lagoon) → 40 on the west side (the Shield, Alaska, the Slab). The level scales
the whole blockout uniformly to landmark size (one `GYM_SCALE`), keeping its proportions.

## Piece format (`kind` → fields)

Every piece has `x, z` (centre, m), `rot` (degrees; a piece faces local +z, so 0 faces +z,
90 faces +x, 180 faces −z, 270 faces −x), `base` (y of its foot; −2.45 = the pit floor),
`name` and `note` (paint and character: read it).

- `sport` / `boulder`: a wall `length` m wide. `profile` is its panels bottom to top, each
  `{len, angle}`: 0° vertical, + overhangs toward the climber, − is a slab. Optional
  `ropes`, `color`; `back` is the depth behind the face.
- `tree`: `width, trunk, caveDepth, caveHeight, height, headAngle`: a trunk in the middle, a
  cave each side overhanging forward to meet the trunk at `caveHeight`, a headwall on top. The trunk is solid
  from the floor up and stands proud of the back wall: the ride runs through the gap between them (#671, `TREE_GAP`).
- `block` (freestanding boulder): footprint `w × d`, corners cut by `chamfer`; `profile`
  bands push every side out (+) or in (−); `color` / `colorB` paint the −x half (body /
  base band), `color2` / `colorB2` the +x half.
- `arch`: `length` span along local x, `d` passage depth along local z, `height`, a
  round-topped opening `opening × openingHeight`, `flare`; `attached: true` = its +x end is
  built into a wall.
- `star`: `points, radius, inner, depth, colorA, colorB`, mounted on a wall face.
- `pit`: `w × d`, `depth` down. `stairs`: the top step at the origin (`base` = top height),
  going down along local +z; `w, steps, rise, run`.
- `floor`: a slab with its top at `base` (a room floor or roof). `pad`: a mat `thick` m
  thick. `prop`: a box `w × d × h`.
- `windows`: `count` windows, each `winW × h`, along `length`, sill at `base`, on a hall
  wall facing in. `autobelay`: the device at `base` (a wall's top), with webbing and a black
  "CLIP IN" flag on the floor below.

## The four ride moments (Alex)

1. Dive into the Starr pit for the Medusa finale, facing the star as she emerges.
2. Thread through the Arch, where it meets the south wall.
3. Spiral around the burning Flame.
4. Pass up the Tree's trunk into its headwall, and skim the glass gym room's windows.
