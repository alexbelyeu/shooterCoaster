# Vision — ShooterCoaster

_Owned by the Director. v0 seeded during setup (2026-10-19); the Director rewrites it
on its first run._

## Elevator pitch

A rollercoaster ride through spectacular themed worlds where you blast everything
that moves. Instantly playable in a browser, gorgeous in motion, tight arcade
scoring that makes you want "one more run".

## Pillars (draft)

1. **Spectacle in motion** — speed, drops, loops and set-pieces; every biome has
   landmarks and moments you remember.
2. **Juicy, readable shooting** — enemies pop against the scenery, every hit feels
   great (feedback, particles, audio, combo escalation, slow-mo).
3. **Arcade mastery** — scores, stars, combos, leaderboards, daily challenges,
   ghosts; short runs, high replay value.
4. **Cohesive stylised look** — code-generated geometry, shaders and post-processing
   with a strong art direction per biome. Polish over realism.
5. **Runs everywhere** — smooth on a mid-range laptop and on phones; fast to load.

## Quality bar

- First 30 seconds: title screen → riding → first kill must feel instant and impressive.
- No visual bugs a player would screenshot (floating props, z-fighting, empty
  stretches, invisible enemies).
- Each level: distinct silhouette, palette, enemies, landmark and music.
