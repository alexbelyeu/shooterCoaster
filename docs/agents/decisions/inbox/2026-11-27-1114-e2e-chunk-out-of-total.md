## 2026-11-27 — The `?e2e=1`-only test hook chunk is listed but not counted in total JS (Director, #1114, #1113)

**Context:** #1113 moves `window.__SC_TEST__` (`src/testing/e2eHook.tsx`) out of the startup JS into a chunk only `?e2e=1` loads: startup JS -19356 B gz, but total JS +3554 B (1642 B over `jsTotalGzipKB` 668), because the hook now compresses alone (8260 B) and the modules it held at startup sit in small shared chunks. No player ever downloads the hook chunk; it ships in `dist/` only because the smoke and prod-check drive the production build with `?e2e=1`.
**Decision:** `scripts/check-bundle.mjs` prints the e2e hook's chunk (identified by its name, `e2eHook-*.js`) but leaves it out of the `jsTotalGzipKB` sum, and fails if that chunk is ever in the startup JS (index.html). `jsTotalGzipKB` stays 668: it measures what a player can download. Without the hook, #1113's branch totals 661.5 KB, below `main`'s 666.1. No budget is raised. Only that one named chunk is excluded; anything else test-only that wants out needs its own entry.
**Consequences:** If the hook chunk is renamed, it is counted again (stricter, never looser). Once #1115 (#1113) and #1002 (#998) are on `main`, the next Director lowers `startupJsGzipKB` to the measured startup + ~2 KB (it should land near 361, from 378/380), so the 19 KB that #1113 frees stays a real saving.
