## 2026-11-11 — The first interior biome is an ordinary biome with a flat floor (builder, #457)
**Context:** #457 asked `BiomeDefinition` to gain "what an interior needs" (no terrain, flat ground, a ceiling) for the Climbing Gym.
**Decision:** It gained nothing. The gym's floor is the shared terrain mesh with a flat `terrainNoise.ts` config (amplitude 0, `baseY` 0, below the track's lowest point by more than `TRACK_CLEARANCE`, so the corridor clamp never dents it); the hall (walls, catwalk, ceiling, skylights) is the biome's `Features`, its holds, volumes and boulders its `Decorations`. The sky dome and its env bake stay: the ceiling hides the dome, the bake lights the walls' reflections, and `atmosphere.ts` `gym` sets the skylights as the "sun" straight above. The hall is sized round level6's track (`biomes/gym/hall.ts`, held by `hall.test.ts`), and the level's climbable `walls` are the hall's lanes (`GYM_WALLS`). A wave can start its climbers higher with `EnemyWave.wallStart`.
**Consequences:** A second interior (the Haunted Mine) copies `biomes/gym/` and needs no host changes. If an interior ever needs the dome or terrain gone (a ceiling with real openings onto a different sky, a sunken floor), that is the time to add a `BiomeDefinition` field, not before.
