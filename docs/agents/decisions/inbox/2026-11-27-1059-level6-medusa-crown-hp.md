## 2026-11-27 — Level 6: the Medusa's hp sits in the crown snake, and her snakes shut between strikes (builder, #1059)

**Context:** #1059 asks for a boss fight of 12 s or more. The Medusa arrives at 40 s. From about 51 s the ride turns so her side snakes (phase 1) leave the frame, and only the crown snake stays in view to the end of the run (`gymTrack.test.ts`, #856). When the hp went into her side snakes (4 / 3, crown 5), or she arrived at 39 s, the fight was not beaten. When she arrived at 38 s, the rider missed the first second of her rise (#926's test).
**Decision:** Her snakes are 3 hp (the first four) and 2 hp (the next four). The crown snake has 12 hp, the most of any weak point, because it is the one the rider can see until the end. A snake is shut (`retract`) while it sways coiled, as long as another snake of its phase is rearing or striking (`snakeShut`). That rule always leaves one snake open, so shutting never leaves the rider with nothing to shoot. The crown snake never shuts. The boss stays at the template's 40 s.
**Consequences:** In the review she lasts 12.7 s, framed 10.9 s of it. To make her fight longer, add hp to the crown, not to the side snakes, and don't move her arrival earlier than 40 s.
