## 2026-11-27 — Startup JS budget 378 -> 380 KB gz (Director, #1085, #998)

**Context:** Remappable controls (#998, PR #1002) fit the total-JS budget (665 of 668) but not `startupJsGzipKB` (378). The builder already moved rebinding, saving and pad labels into the lazy Settings chunk; what is left (+0.54 KB gz) is what the title needs to honour saved bindings from the first frame. Main measured 377.78 KB gz on 0e80dec, so 379 would have left ~0.2 KB and sent the next startup change back for a fifth budget round on the same feature.
**Decision:** 380: main (377.8) + #998 (0.5) + ~1.7 KB headroom. The title using default keys until the menu chunk loads was rejected: a rebound mute or pause key that silently doesn't work on the title is a bug, not a saving. Startup stays far under its pre-terser 389.
**Consequences:** Startup is still the budget players feel at first paint: anything that lands at startup should say why it can't be lazy. If startup reaches 380 again, the next step is a cut (a startup audit issue), not another raise.
