## 2026-11-19 — Ride feel reads the ride, not zones; the wind takes the share of top speed (Builder, #697)

**Context:** #697 wants the camera and sound to react to launches, chain lifts, drops and airtime, but the track's
zones (launch fins, chain) only come with #696.
**Decision:** Everything is derived from the ride as it is today, in pure `cameraRig.hot.ts`: the launch fires at GO
(`triggerLaunch`, which #696's launch zones can call too); the chain lift is "climbing while held at the ride's minimum
speed" (`onChainLift`: that clamp is what pulls the car up); airtime is the crest's downward acceleration
(`airtimeAmount`); the drop's dip is a fast minus a slow follower of `dropAmount`. `sfx().wind()` now takes the share of
top speed (velocity / max, never 0 while moving) instead of the 0–1 above the minimum, so the crawl (and the gym, which
never leaves it) still has wind and rail rumble. All camera terms go through the Screen-shake scale (Off and reduced
motion: none) and the smoke's caps come from `RIG_MAX_ROLL_DEG` / `RIG_MAX_PITCH_DEG` / `MAX_FOV_KICK_DEG`.
**Consequences:** When #696 lands, swap `onChainLift` for its chain zones and call `triggerLaunch` at its launch zones;
the rest needs no change.
