## 2026-11-19 — The v2 ride is a solved plan, ridden by time (builder, #696)

**Context:** Real speed (gravity, launches, lift hills, brakes) changes when the rider reaches each part of the
track, but a level's timed beats (waves, the boss at 40 s) must still land on their seconds, and spawning predicts the
ride ahead (`rideProfile`).
**Decision:** `rideV2.ts` simulates the whole ride from GO once per level mount (60 samples a second, 240 s) and the
live camera reads it by ride time instead of integrating per frame. Between two storyboard beats the solver has one
knob, a scale `a` on every speed (zone targets, crawl) and `a²` on every acceleration (gravity, a zone's), drag per
speed² unchanged: from rest that is the same ride exactly 1 / a as long, so the ride keeps its shape (slow crests, fast
drops) while the beat moves onto its second. Opt-in per level (`TrackParams.ride: 'v2'`); today's rule is untouched.
**Consequences:** The ride and its prediction are identical at any frame rate. Gravity can differ between two beats'
stretches (invisible on a ride, but a stretch asked to be much faster or slower than it rolls feels floaty or heavy:
place beats the track can roughly reach). The smoke's `rideSpeed` override has nothing to hold on a v2 level.
