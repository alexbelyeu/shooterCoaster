  7 of its 9 completes. Each of the four visitor-days started level 6, which only unlocks in a
  browser that has a star on levels 1 to 5, in the days after the gym rebuild and the ride kit
  landed and while Alex's level 6 replay is due (#671). Neither phone carries the internal flag.
  If they are players, they are returning ones who finished the campaign; either way they are not
  new arrivals.
- **Strangers this week: 2 people, 3 starts, 1 complete.** Australia on Linux (18 Nov: level1 and
  level2, one finished at 3★) and India on Android (20 Nov: one start on level3, so a browser
  that had played before; no complete). Last week it was 4 people, 7 starts, 2 completes. Of 13
  visitor-days that are neither a US iPhone nor a Mac, 2 started a level (15 %; W46 21 %, W44
  19 %).
- **Eight Windows visitor-days in the US, ten views, no level started.** Three of them on Mon
  16 Nov alone. Windows was 11 visitor-days with one start last week. People who land and leave,
  or something automated; the API gives no way to tell.
- **Search went back to zero.** One google.com visit in W46, none in W47. The SEO series now
  reads 1, 0.
- **Quits appeared (4, after 0 last week), all from the two iPhones on 21 Nov.** Two are level1
  at 0 % of the timer. Since #674 (19 Nov) a start is counted when the loading screen opens,
  before the 10 s intro tour, so a 0 % quit is someone who left in the first seconds of the ride.
  Two events from phones that are probably the studio's own is not a finding; if the first posts'
  players do the same, the intro tour is where to look.
- **3★ is still the usual result: 7 of 9 completes** (16 of 16 last week). The two 1★ runs are a
  Mac on level1 and an iPhone.
- **Mobile start rate (experiment 3):** 5 of 6 mobile visitor-days started a level, but four of
  those are the US iPhones. Counting only the other two: 1 of 2. Since 22 Oct, 4 of 26 without
  the US iPhones.
- **Counting changes inside this week:** Restart from the pause menu sends `level_start` (#588,
  16 Nov); a start is counted at the loading screen, not the countdown (#674, 19 Nov), which
  lowers the completion rate for the same play; `level_start` carries `from` (#811, 21 Nov).
