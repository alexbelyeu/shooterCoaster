## What it means

1. **The baseline is flat and small — about 5 visitors and 1 level start a day — and that is
   fine: it is the ruler.** W44 was the first week with Alex's devices excluded and no setup
   traffic, so it is the first honest number. A Showcase post that brings 100 visitors in a
   week would be a 3× jump against it and unmistakable in the daily table.
2. **Everyone who gets this far is still coming from a direct link; nobody has arrived from a
   search engine or a shared score yet.** Search needs #274 (Alex) and time; the share link has
   had one chance to be used (one completed run since it shipped) and wasn't. Neither is a
   finding yet.
3. **Engagement is the number to carry forward, not visitors: 19 % of visitors started a level
   (desktop 21 %, mobile 11 %), 38 % of starts finished.** Both are down on W43's 44 %, but W43's
   completes were 4 people (some of them Alex pre-flag) and W44's are 3, so the honest reading is
   "a quarter to a half of the few who press PLAY finish a run" and "four of five visitors never
   press PLAY". The second one is what the title screen has to fix, and it is also what the
   Showcase thread will tell us about: 100 visitors × 19 % = 19 starts would be a real sample.
   Mobile start rate (experiment 3): 1 of 9 this week, 1 of 18 over two weeks. Below the 20
   mobile visitors the experiment asked for, so **still no P1**, but phones are now 11 % vs
   desktop 21 % (10 of 50 desktop visitors over two weeks); the first post with real mobile volume decides it.
