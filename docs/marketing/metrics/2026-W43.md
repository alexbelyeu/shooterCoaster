## What it means

1. **Measurement works.** Page views, devices, countries and the per-level funnel all read
   from the API; the weekly file will have real numbers from here on. The one hole is
   campaign attribution: UTM breakdowns are paywalled, so every link we hand out must be
   identifiable by **referrer** (fine for forum/Reddit posts, useless for bare re-shares,
   messaging apps and QR codes) or by **path** — hence `/go/<campaign>` landing paths. The
   game already serves at any path (`vercel.json` catch-all rewrite; verified with a local
   preview + headless Chromium this run), so #102's link switched to `/go/showcase` today and
   #171 (P2) pins the behaviour with a smoke step and keeps `/go/` out of the index.
2. **The desktop funnel looks healthy for the handful who played: 18 starts → 8 completes
   (44 %) from 4 people, half of the completed runs at 3★,** and people go on to level 2 and 3.
   Too few people to mean anything yet, but nothing says "the game is broken".
3. **Mobile is the thing to watch.** 29 % of visitors came on a phone and **none of them
   started a level**. With 9 visitors, some of them Alex's own pre-flag devices and some
   probably link previews, this is not yet evidence — but if W44 shows ≥ 20 mobile visitors
   and still ~0 `level_start`, the title → PLAY → level select path on phones is the first
   thing to fix before any portal upload (segment A is mobile-heavy). No new event is filed
   for it yet; the ratio `level_start` / mobile visitors is the signal.
