  an iPhone (12 Nov, 3 starts and 2 completes at 3★, the same day as the google.com referral),
  the Netherlands on Windows (1 start), China on Android (1 start). Of 19 non-Mac visitor-days,
  4 started a level (21 %), in line with W44's 19 %. Small, but it is the first sign that people
  who arrive unprompted press PLAY, and that a phone player finishes runs.
- **Mobile start rate (experiment 3): 2 of 5 this week, 3 of 24 since 22 Oct** (0 of 9, 1 of 9,
  0 of 1, 2 of 5). The 20-visitor bar is passed with a rate no worse than desktop's this week
  (2 of 14 non-Mac desktop), so no P1 on the phone path. Volume decides it properly.
- **Search exists now.** #274 (Search Console + Bing) was closed by Alex on 9 Nov; one google.com
  visit followed on 12 Nov. One visit is not a trend; it is the first row of the SEO channel.
- **Every complete was 3★ (16 of 16), including the iPhone player's two.** Mostly Alex, so not a
  finding yet, but with the 60 s rules (#434) new this week it is worth the Director's eye: if
  first-time players also 3★ every level, there is nothing to replay for.
- **Counting changes this week, so W47 will read higher for the same play:** #588 (16 Nov) makes
  Restart from the pause menu send `level_start`; before it, a restarted run was not counted.
